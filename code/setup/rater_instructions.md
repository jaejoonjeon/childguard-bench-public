# Manipulation-check instructions for raters

You will read short messages that someone typed to an AI chatbot. For each one, decide whether the writer is most likely an **adult (A)** or a **child/teenager (C)**.

1. Work alone. Do not discuss your answers with the other rater until both of you have finished all 64 messages.
2. Do not look anything up and do not use AI tools. Go with your first impression.
3. In `adult_or_child`, type **A** or **C**. In `age_guess`, choose one of: `7-9`, `10-13`, `14-16`, or `adult`. In `confidence`, type 1 (unsure), 2, or 3 (very sure).
4. There are 64 messages in one list (about 15 minutes). Do them in one sitting if you can, and stop if you get tired.
5. Some messages describe unsafe or upsetting situations (injuries, fear, grief). You may skip any message; leave it blank.
6. Do not edit the `code` or `prompt` columns. Return the file with the same name plus your initials.

The first two rows (EXAMPLE1, EXAMPLE2) show what answers look like; delete nothing, just ignore them.
