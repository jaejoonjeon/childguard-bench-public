## Human audit outcome (Oct 25, 2026)

The registered audit of 100 first-run responses was scored by hand, blind to model and judge labels. The sheet first came back with R016 blank (kappa 0.10 on the 99 scored rows, agreement 78.8%). About six minutes later the researcher saved a second copy that is identical except R016 = N, entered after being told the row was missing, not blind to that fact. Both versions are reported. On all 100 rows, with R016 = N, agreement with the final judge labels was 79.0% and Cohen's kappa for strict violation was 0.10 (bootstrap 95% interval −0.11 to 0.34). The registered threshold is 0.70, so under the registration the primary results are reported as exploratory and the judge-reliability problem is stated. No judge re-run or re-audit is triggered, since those steps apply only to kappa from 0.70 to below 0.80. This is the registered rule applied, not a deviation.

Sources of disagreement found on review: scoring notes show some Y labels judged the grading rules instead of the responses (12 rows where the researcher said violation and the judges did not); in 9 rows the judges said violation and the researcher did not, with some judge calls defensible and some looking wrong; a few items have unclear rules (R072 and R089 adult versions, R081, R077). Items are not corrected or re-scored. No further audit was run (see below).

## Second audit: planned Oct 25, not run (updated Oct 28, 2026)

On Oct 25 a second audit was planned as an addition to the registration. The registration only allows a second audit after the judge prompt is clarified (kappa 0.70 to below 0.80), so it would have been a deviation, and it could not have changed the registered outcome. The researcher then decided not to run it, so there is no second audit.

Why it was considered: the researcher reported that he had misread the first sheet's instruction and judged whether the grading rules were right instead of whether each response matched the violation description. This is reported as a likely source of disagreement in the first audit. The first audit stands as scored.

What was done before the decision: a sample of 100 new first-run responses was drawn with the first audit's procedure, excluding the first sample (random seed 20261025, recorded here before the draw). While preparing the draw, the number of judge-flagged violations in it (4) was seen. No scoring sheet was built and no response in that sample was scored.

Result: the first audit (kappa 0.10) is the only human audit. The primary results are reported as exploratory, as the registration requires.
